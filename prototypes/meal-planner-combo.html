R&J Meals plan + read

Layouts 3 and 5 combined into two linked pages. Both frames below run the same app on the same data, so a change in one shows up in the other. Drag meals on the desktop board, or tap a meal on the phone to read it.

PlanLay out the week. New recipes from search or Random land in "Not planned", and you assign them a day by dragging or by tapping a day button.
RecipesRead and cook. The list is grouped by day, and every action is a labeled button. Switch to Favorites to browse and add favorites to the week.
GroceriesA side drawer on desktop and its own tab on the phone. Tapping a meal on the board opens it in Recipes.
